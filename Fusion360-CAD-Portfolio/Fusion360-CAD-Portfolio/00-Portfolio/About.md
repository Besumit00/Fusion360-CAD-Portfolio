# About Me

## Who I Am

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from **Madhav Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Gwalior**, working toward a career as a **Robotics Design Engineer**. This repository is my structured record of learning Fusion 360 from first principles and applying it to real robotics projects.

## Why This Portfolio Exists

Most CAD "portfolios" I found online were just folders of unrelated tutorial files with no context. I wanted mine to show:

1. **Progression** — how my modeling ability grew from a single extruded box to a 6-DOF robotic arm assembly.
2. **Reasoning** — not just what I built, but *why* I made specific design decisions, what went wrong, and how I fixed it.
3. **Direction** — that every skill here is being deliberately built toward a robotics/mechatronics design career, not learned in isolation.

## Core Interests

- Mechanical design for robotics (links, joints, actuator mounts)
- CAD-to-simulation workflows (Fusion 360 → URDF → ROS2/Gazebo)
- Manufacturing-aware design (DFM for 3D printing, sheet metal, and CNC machining)
- Parametric, configurable part design
